A simple checklist before your appointment

A little preparation can help you explain your needs and understand the next steps. Consider bringing or noting:
- Your current medications and supplements
- Relevant medical history and any concerns you want to raise
- Your weight or wellness goals, in your own words
- What approaches you have tried and how they went
- Questions about nutrition counseling, treatment choices, and follow-up

Avoid starting a prescription medication, hormone treatment, or supplement based on someone else’s experience. Also avoid leaving out health details because they seem unrelated. Sharing complete information helps the team have a more useful discussion with you.
